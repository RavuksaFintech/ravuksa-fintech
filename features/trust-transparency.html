1. About RAVUKSA FINTECH

RAVUKSA FINTECH is a Financial Education & Market Learning platform designed to help people understand financial concepts, market fundamentals and responsible money management in simple language.

Our objective is to make financial knowledge more accessible through educational content, interactive tools and learning resources.

We aim to support learning from beginner to advanced levels through simple explanations and practical educational experiences.

2. Our Educational Mission

We believe informed financial decisions begin with better understanding.

  • Promote financial literacy and awareness.
  • Explain financial concepts in simple language.
  • Encourage responsible market learning.
  • Provide educational calculators and simulators.
  • Help users understand financial risks.
  • Encourage verification through official sources.

3. Market Data Transparency

Certain market information displayed on RAVUKSA FINTECH may be obtained through third-party data providers, including IndianAPI where configured.

Market information may be delayed, incomplete, unavailable or subject to provider updates.

  • Data availability depends on the provider.
  • Displayed values should be independently verified.
  • Historical performance does not guarantee future results.
  • RAVUKSA FINTECH does not guarantee data accuracy or uninterrupted availability.
Important: Always verify important market information through official exchange sources before relying on it.

4. Financial Risk Disclosure

Financial markets involve risk. Investments and market activities may result in financial losses.

RAVUKSA FINTECH provides educational information only. Nothing on this platform should be interpreted as personalised investment advice, a buy/sell recommendation or a promise of returns.

  • Understand risks before making financial decisions.
  • Do not rely only on educational examples or calculators.
  • Consider your financial situation and risk tolerance.
  • Consult an appropriately qualified professional when necessary.

5. Privacy & User Responsibility

Users should access the platform responsibly and avoid sharing passwords, OTPs, banking credentials or other confidential information.

Any personal information submitted through platform features should be handled according to the applicable privacy practices and policies of RAVUKSA FINTECH.

Users are responsible for independently verifying information before taking financial or other important decisions.
