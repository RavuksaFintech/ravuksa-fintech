1. About This Policy

RAVUKSA FINTECH is a Financial Education & Market Learning platform. This Privacy Policy explains the information that may be processed when users access or interact with our website and its features.

By using the platform, users should review this policy and understand the information practices described here.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features used, the platform may process information such as:

  • Name or username provided by the user.
  • Email address used for account registration or login.
  • Profile information voluntarily submitted by the user.
  • Learning activity and topic completion progress.
  • Information submitted through interactive platform features.
  • Basic technical information processed by hosting or service providers.

The information processed may vary depending on the features available and the services used.

3. Account & Firebase Services

Certain account and learning features of RAVUKSA FINTECH use Google Firebase services.

Depending on the enabled configuration, Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore may process account information, profile details and learning progress.

Such information is used to support account access, profile functionality and learning-related features.

Firebase services are subject to Google's applicable privacy and security documentation.

Official reference: Firebase Privacy and Security

4. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide account and profile functionality.
  • Save and display learning progress.
  • Improve website features and user experience.
  • Maintain platform functionality and security.
  • Respond to user enquiries and support requests.
  • Understand and troubleshoot technical issues.

5. Third-Party Services

RAVUKSA FINTECH may use third-party services to support website hosting, authentication, database functionality and market information.

The website and its service providers may use browser storage, cookies or similar technologies where required for website functionality.

These technologies may support account sessions, website preferences, offline functionality or other enabled features.

Available controls may depend on the user's browser and device settings.

Users should never share passwords, OTPs, banking credentials or confidential financial information through public forms or messages.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate enabled website features.

We do not claim that information is never shared or retained. Actual handling depends on the services used, their configuration and applicable legal requirements.

Users should contact us for questions about information associated with their accounts.

Users may contact RAVUKSA FINTECH to ask questions about their information or request assistance concerning account-related data.
